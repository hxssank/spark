Display fit and removal details

Charge and health are different readings

Charge is energy remaining now. Capacity health compares a learned usable capacity with a matching baseline. A new or unidentified cell shows "Health unknown". A stale sensor reading shows "Sensor fault".

The planned MAX17260 gauge must stay paired with its cartridge, or the host must identify the replacement cell. A two-wire A30 connection cannot provide that identity. A generic 10 mΩ gauge module clips above 5.12 A; the shunt and current range must match this design.
